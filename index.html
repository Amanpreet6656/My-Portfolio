Smart Kanji Analyzer

Personal Tool • Japanese Study Utility

Tool

A custom web tool I built to solve my own bottleneck while studying Japanese: stopping mid-reading to manually look up unknown kanji killed my study flow.

How It Works

Parses raw Japanese text directly in the browser, isolating individual characters and compound words. It maps each character against structured kanji datasets to display Onyomi/Kunyomi readings, radical breakdowns, meanings, and JLPT levels instantly without page reloads.

Zero dependencies, instant local parsing, and designed purely around efficient study workflow.
