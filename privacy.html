Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the data processing standards of the professional engineering portfolio operated by Amanpreet Singh (hereinafter "the Operator"). This web platform functions as a client-side portfolio application for evaluation by university computer science admissions faculty (specifically including institutions in Japan such as the University of Aizu), research laboratories, and technical collaborators.

2. Zero Data Collection Architecture

In accordance with the Operator's offline-first engineering philosophy, this website operates with zero user data ingestion. Specifically:

  • No tracking cookies, marketing beacons, or session fingerprinting mechanisms are loaded.
  • No third-party analytics telemetry (such as Google Analytics or tracking pixels) is embedded.
  • No authentication databases or server-side user data stores exist.
  • No personal data submitted via email protocols is shared or distributed to commercial third parties.
  • Application state operations remain isolated within the local client sandbox.

3. Local Storage Usage

The application interacts with the browser's Web Storage API (localStorage) exclusively to persist user-selected theme parameters (Dark Mode versus Light Mode). This local key-value entry:

  • Contains strictly monochromatic interface identifiers.
  • Remains isolated on the visitor's local hardware device.
  • Is never transmitted across any network boundary.
  • May be cleared at any time via standard browser cache purging routines.

4. External Content Delivery Networks

External font assets and script bundles (GSAP, Anime.js, and JetBrains Mono) are loaded via verified public CDNs (Google Fonts, cdnjs.cloudflare.com). Resource requests to these servers are subject to the respective infrastructure privacy guidelines of Google LLC and Cloudflare, Inc.

5. Direct Communications

Inquiries initiated through the contact interface are handled directly via standard electronic mail protocols (RFC 5322). Email correspondence sent to amanpreetsingh21605@gmail.com is retained strictly for professional and academic evaluation purposes and is never monetized.

6. Governing Standards

This policy reflects strict technical data minimization principles compliant with international data protection frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Japanese Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).
