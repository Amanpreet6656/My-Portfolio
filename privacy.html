Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the data processing standards of the professional engineering portfolio operated by Amanpreet Singh (hereinafter "the Operator"). This web platform functions as a client-side portfolio application for evaluation by university admissions committees, research laboratories, and technical evaluators.

2. Zero Data Collection Architecture

In accordance with the Operator's offline-first engineering philosophy, this website operates without collecting, storing, or processing personal data. Specifically:

  • No tracking cookies, marketing beacons, or session fingerprinting mechanisms are loaded.
  • No third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or tracking pixels) are embedded.
  • No user accounts, authentication systems, or server-side user databases exist.
  • No personal information submitted via direct correspondence is sold, shared, or distributed to third parties.
  • All application state and client operations remain strictly isolated within the visitor's local browser environment.

3. Local Storage Usage

The application interacts with the browser's Web Storage API (localStorage) solely to persist user-selected interface preferences (such as light or dark display mode). This entry:

  • Contains strictly non-personal interface state identifiers.
  • Remains isolated on the visitor's local device.
  • Is never transmitted across network boundaries or shared with external servers.
  • May be cleared at any time by deleting browser cache and site data.

4. External Content Delivery Networks

External font assets and script libraries may be loaded via public Content Delivery Networks (such as Google Fonts or cdnjs). Requests to these third-party CDN endpoints are governed by the respective privacy policies and infrastructure terms of those providers.

5. Direct Communications

Inquiries initiated through the contact interface are handled directly via standard email communication. Correspondence sent to amanpreetsingh21605@gmail.com is retained solely for professional and academic evaluation purposes and is never sold, monetized, or shared with third parties.

6. Regulatory Compliance

This policy reflects data minimization principles consistent with international data protection frameworks, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable personal data protection laws.
